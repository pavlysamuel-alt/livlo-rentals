Rivertrail Retreat

Sleeps 104 bedrooms · 5 beds3 bathrooms★ 4.96 · 158 reviews

A chef's kitchen in black, a theatre room that seats everyone, and a deck that looks straight out over the park.

Rivertrail Retreat is a four-bedroom house in Kitchener built for groups who would rather cook than go out. The kitchen is the centre of it — black cabinetry, hidden drawers, and a spice drawer guests photograph before they have finished unpacking.

The theatre room seats the whole party, popcorn machine included. Upstairs the bedrooms carry marble accents and one bathroom holds a freestanding tub. Four bedrooms, five beds, three full bathrooms, sleeping ten.

Outside, the deck is the reason people book: a pergola, a sunken fire pit, a barbecue, and a view straight over the park. The airport is close enough to watch the planes come in, and the ski hills are an easy winter drive.
