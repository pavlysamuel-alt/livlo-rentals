Palmasis Oasis

Sleeps 104 bedrooms · 5 beds3 bathrooms★ 4.98 · 59 reviews

A canal at the end of the lawn, a pool under screen, and a kitchen built for the kind of evening nobody wants to end.

Palmasis Oasis sits on a quiet residential canal in Palm Coast. The pool is private and screened, the dock is yours alone, and between April and July the dolphins come through — manatees take their turn from September.

Inside are four bedrooms, five beds and three renovated bathrooms. The living room is arranged around an 85-inch screen on a concrete floating wall, with 5.1 surround and a cinema popcorn machine that guests mention more often than almost anything else in the house.

Outdoors, the cooking station runs twelve feet: a four-burner grill, a wood-fired pizza oven and a sink, with a covered table long enough for everyone. The beach is fifteen minutes away, St. Augustine twenty, and James F. Holland Memorial Park a three-minute drive.
